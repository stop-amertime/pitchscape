Where each detector stops printing. The starting values are the detectors’ own settings.

Voice

How sure the pitch tracker must be that it hears a sung note. Lower prints more of the voice, and also breaths and bleed from other instruments. Its own setting: 45%.
Singing quieter than this, as a share of the song’s loud singing, doesn’t print. Its own setting: 4%.
Bits of voice shorter than this don’t print. Its own setting: 90 ms.

Bass

Bass quieter than this, as a share of the song’s loud bass, doesn’t print. Its own setting: 8%.

Notes

A note keeps printing while the note detector is at least this sure it is still sounding. Lower lets notes ring on; higher cuts them short. Its own setting: 30%.
Notes shorter than this don’t print. Its own setting: 100 ms.
A note on a harmonic of a lower note prints only if it stands at least this many dB above that note’s neighbouring harmonics, so overtones don’t print as notes of their own. Default: 7 dB.
A note at a pitch that is still ringing joins the ringing one unless its sound jumps at least this many dB as it starts. Default: 6 dB.
Notes and lines fade to nothing this far below the instrument’s loud level. Default: 50 dB.

Drums

How sure the drum detector must be before a hit prints, as a multiple of its own settings. Lower prints quieter, ghostlier hits.

Noise and strikes

Noise (hiss, breath, distortion: what is neither tone nor strike) tears a note’s edge and grows bristles when it comes within this many dB of the loudest sound near it. Higher shows quieter noise. Default: 24 dB.
Noise only shows where at least this share of an instrument’s whole sound is noise (fully at twice this), so a clean piano stays clean. Default: 6%.
How far a fully noisy note’s edge is torn up and down. Default: 1.3 semitones.
A strike (a pick, a slap, a consonant, a muted chop) prints as fine hatching when it comes within this many dB of the loudest sound near it. Default: 12 dB.

Lines

The guitar’s melody as one line (a solo, a bend), heard by the voice’s pitch tracker, over its notes.
How sure the pitch tracker must be that it hears the guitar’s melody before its line prints. Its own setting: 21%.
A voice or bass line that jumps more than this many semitones at once starts a new stroke. Default: 4.
